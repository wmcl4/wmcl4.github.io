Satellite imaging passes

Times when an Earth-observation satellite's footprint covers a location. This shows opportunity, not confirmed imaging.

Pick location on map

Click the map or drag the pin. The coordinates and time zone update automatically.

Map tiles by OpenStreetMap contributors; time zones from Open-Meteo. Each time is the moment the satellite is closest to you; your location is inside its swath around then. Orbits come from CelesTrak and are propagated from the latest elements, so passes far from the data date are less accurate. Swath widths are nominal; check mission documentation.
