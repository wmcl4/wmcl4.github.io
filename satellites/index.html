Satellite Overpass Times

This page looks up when Landsat and Sentinel Earth-observation satellites pass over a place. You give it a latitude and longitude (or click on a map) and a start date, and it makes a list of passes with the local time.

Notes

  • A pass is the moment a satellite is level with your location while your location is inside its swath. It shows when the satellite could image you, not that it did.
  • Optical satellites need daylight, so night passes are left out unless you turn them on. Radar works at night.
  • Swath widths are nominal. Check each mission's documentation for exact numbers.
  • Passes far from today are less accurate, because the orbits are calculated from the most recent published data.
